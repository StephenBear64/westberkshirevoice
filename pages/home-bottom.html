West Berkshire Voice is YOUR new community media project, providing news, information and entertainment within our area. We are a not-for-profit organisation funded only by sponsorship and donations. The website, audio and video content is produced by YOU, the community. We have no full-time journalists, creators or staff, so we’re looking for anyone interested in media production or journalism to contact us and get involved. Everyone is welcome, regardless of skills and experience, and we have team members who will provide training and help you learn and feel part of the team. The quantity and quality of our content depend on YOU, so please do pitch in and help.
