About West Berkshire Voice

West Berkshire Voice is a not-for-profit community organisation established to provide a voice for everyone in West Berkshire. Our volunteer team creates online and social media content and podcasts, as well as scheduled live audio and video streams.

Our output includes news, information and entertainment for our West Berkshire audience, produced by YOU, the people of West Berkshire.

Our mission is to empower West Berkshire people, whether they are in towns or villages. Our digital service provides local news, fosters engaging discussions and offers entertaining and informative content.

Our team supports charities and other voluntary organisations by promoting their events and appeals, as well as providing training in digital skills, helping them get the most from social media and the internet.

We have a special focus on people who are disabled or have a long-term illness. We aim to support those who are housebound or have mobility issues. We are committed to inclusion, and we want to help end isolation and loneliness in our community.

At West Berkshire Voice, we foster a welcoming and inclusive environment where individuals can come together, learn new skills, and feel a sense of belonging as they contribute to something meaningful and exciting.

For our project to be successful, we need people with ideas for news stories, podcasts and programmes who want to have a go at producing them. So if you have an idea or want to try writing, photography, filming or producing, then do get in touch and we will help you turn your ideas into published content. This is YOUR community media project, so we hope you will get involved.
