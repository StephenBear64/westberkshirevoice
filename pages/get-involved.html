West BerkshireVoiceNeedsYou!

Everything you see, hear and watch from West Berkshire Voice, from the news stories and pictures to the videos, podcasts and live streams, is made by local people who had an idea and decided to give it a go.

Maybe you've got a great idea for a story, a podcast or a show, and you'd like help bringing it to life. Or maybe you fancy learning to be a producer or a citizen journalist. Either way, there's room for you here.

You don't need any experience, because we'll train you in everything from journalism to the technical side. We're all volunteers, so there's no fixed commitment, and you give as much time as you can. All you need to bring is a few ideas, a sense of fun and a willingness to learn.

It's friendly, it's inclusive, you'll meet new people, and it's a lot of fun too.

Up for it? Just fill in our contact form, and we'll be straight back in touch.
