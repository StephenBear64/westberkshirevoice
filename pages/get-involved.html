Get involved

West Berkshire Voice is made by local people. If you have something to say about where you live, we would like to hear it.

What to send

The best way to start is to send us something finished:

  • a short audio recording, which you can make on a phone
  • a short written piece about your town or village

It could be about a local event, a piece of local history, a person worth hearing from, or anything else that matters where you live. You do not need any experience.

How to send it

Email it to hello@westberkshirevoice.uk with your name and the place it is about. We read and listen to everything we receive, but we cannot promise to publish every piece.
