West Berkshire Voice is a not-for-profit community organisation delivering a mixture of news, information, talk, entertainment and music made by the people of West Berkshire for the people of West Berkshire. Our mission is to support local voluntary organisations, promote social inclusion of vulnerable people and to eliminate loneliness and isolation in our community. We are an inclusive organisation and everyone is welcome to get involved creating great online, audio and video content for our audience.
