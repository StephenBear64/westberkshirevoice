About West Berkshire Voice

West Berkshire Voice is an independent community media service for the whole of West Berkshire.

What we do

We make audio programmes, interviews and features about life in the district. You can listen on Mixcloud at any time, and we share updates on Facebook.

How we work

We are run by volunteers. There is no fixed schedule: we publish when there is something worth hearing. Everyone is welcome to listen and to take part.

Where to find us

  • West Berkshire Voice on Mixcloud
  • West Berkshire Voice on Facebook

Get involved

West Berkshire Voice is made by local people. If you have something to say about your town or village, we would like to hear it.
